What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that can generate new data or content—such as text, code, images, audio, or video—after being trained on large datasets. It does not “think” or “understand” like humans do, but it predicts what is likely to come next based on patterns it has learned.

Because of this, GenAI is powerful but also imperfect: it can produce impressive outputs, but it can also be wrong, biased, or misleading if used carelessly.
